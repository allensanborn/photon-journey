One Photon

A single continuous zoom from a sunlit leaf down to the four manganese atoms that take an oxygen molecule out of water. Nine orders of magnitude. No cuts.

10-1 m → 10-10 m  ·  about two minutes

You are breathing it now.

Every O₂ molecule in the air in your lungs was pulled out of water by a cluster of four manganese atoms half a nanometre wide, driven by four photons, in about a millisecond. The chloroplasts of the world do this on the order of 1011 tonnes a year.

The photon that started this left the Sun's surface 8 minutes and 20 seconds ago. The energy it carried had been working its way out of the core for tens of thousands of years before that.

2 H₂O → O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻
